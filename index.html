Hi! I am Zhengyu Hu🤗

Incoming Ph.D. @ University of Washington (Fall 2026)

My research focuses on Data-Centric AI for Large Language Models (LLMs) and Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) (e.g., Image / Video).

I am an incoming Ph.D. student in the ECE department at the University of Washington, Seattle (Fall 2026). I get my M.Phil. degree at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Sep. 2023 – Jul. 2026), advised by Prof. Hui Xiong. I also work closely with Jieyu Zhang at the University of Washington.

I am currently a research intern at Qwen Team, Foundation Model Group. Working for the Qwen3.5 / 3.6 / … series Previously, I interned at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA), supervised by Dr. Xing Xie, Dr. Nicholas Jing Yuan, and Dr. Jianxun Lian. Before that, I interned at Meituan (Foundation Model Group) under the supervision of Dr. Jingang Wang and Dr. Zhengyu Chen.
